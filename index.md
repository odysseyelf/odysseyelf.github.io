Hello, I'm Chih-Chieh Yu, a plant systematist and biogeographer currently working at the Field Museum in Chicago.

Trained as a plant systematist and inspired by years of hiking and botanical exploration, I study how mountain landscapes, climatic change, and ecological traits shape the evolution and distribution of plant diversity.
