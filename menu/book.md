My academic research and science writing share the same goal: helping people understand how plants, mountains, and evolutionary history shape the natural world. Alongside my scientific publications, I have authored popular science books and translated works that introduce natural history and evolutionary thinking to a broader audience.

---

# Popular Science Books

## *THE ODYSSEY OF TAIWAN’S MONTANE PLANTS*

<img src="/assets/img/book1.jpg" alt="Book One" style="width:300px; max-width:100%; border-radius:6px; margin-bottom:20px;">

**Chinese title:** 《通往世界的植物：臺灣高山植物的時空旅史》

**Publisher:** 春山出版社｜**Year:** 2020

Taiwan’s mountains – with their subtropical foothills and alpine peaks – have been a haven for rare plants throughout the island’s history. But how did they get there? Scientist Yu Chih Chieh leads us through millennia of natural history in one of Asia’s great floral ecosystems.

## *RANGES FAR AND HIGH: ON THE PHYTOGEOGRAPHICAL ORIGIN OF TAIWAN’S MONTANE PLANTS*

<img src="/assets/img/book2.jpg" alt="Book Two" style="width:300px; max-width:100%; border-radius:6px; margin-bottom:20px;">

**Chinese title:** 《橫斷臺灣：追尋臺灣高山植物地理起源》

**Publisher:** 春山出版社｜**Year:** 2023

A biogeographic exploration spanning millions of years, this book investigates the origins of Taiwan’s flora and fauna and the island’s incredible biodiversity. Fascinating information is interspersed with insights on the beauty of the natural world.
