My research integrates phylogenomics, systematics, historical biogeography, and functional ecology to understand how mountain floras originate, diversify, and persist through time. Although my study systems span multiple plant groups, they are united by a common goal: uncovering the evolutionary processes that generate biodiversity in mountain ecosystems.

Rather than presenting a complete publication list here, I highlight representative publications organized by research themes.
